Egg Fried Rice

Description

Egg fried rice is a popular dish made with cooked rice, eggs, and various vegetables or meats. It's a quick and easy meal that can be customized based on personal preferences and available ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup frozen peas and carrots
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp garlic powder

Steps

  1. Heat vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Beat the eggs and pour into the skillet.
  3. Cook until the eggs are set, then stir in the cooked rice.
  4. Add the frozen vegetables and soy sauce.
  5. Stir everything together until heated through.
