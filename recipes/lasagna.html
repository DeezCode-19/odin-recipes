Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish that consists of layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese. It is a hearty and comforting meal that is perfect for family dinners or special occasions. The dish is typically made with ground beef or pork, tomato sauce, and a variety of cheeses such as ricotta, mozzarella, and Parmesan. The layers of pasta are alternated with the meat sauce and cheese, creating a delicious and satisfying meal that is loved by many around the world.

Ingredients

  • 500g ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • Lasagna noodles
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Cook the ground beef in a pan until browned.
  3. Add onion and cook until soft.
  4. Pour in tomato sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Layer sauce, noodles, and cheese in a baking dish.
  6. Repeat layers until ingredients are used up.
  7. Bake for 30–40 minutes until golden.
  8. Let it cool before serving.
