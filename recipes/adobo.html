Adobo

Description

Adobo is a popular Filipino dish that is made with meat, usually chicken or pork,

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken or pork, cut into pieces
  • 1 cup vinegar
  • 1 cup soy sauce
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp paprika

Steps

  1. In a large pot, combine vinegar, soy sauce, onion, garlic, black pepper, and paprika.
  2. Bring the mixture to a boil.
  3. Add the meat and reduce heat to low.
  4. Cover and simmer for 30 minutes or until the meat is tender.
  5. Remove from heat and let it cool before serving.
